02 Grout Take by Anchor Drill Log V3.1.3

Three anchors exceeded the 1.35 elevated-take threshold (BEN-SA-003, BEN-SA-008, BEN-SA-014), each correlating with a logged fractured zone or cavity in the ground-conditions record. Elevated take is expected behaviour in those conditions and was fully returned to surface; no anchor showed unexplained loss.

Ground conditions summary

The works passed through the concrete spillway cap into Torlesse greywacke, weathering from highly to moderately weathered in the upper holes and grading to slightly weathered and fresh rock in the bond zones. Discrete fractured zones between 4 m and 8 m drove the elevated grout take on the flagged anchors. Groundwater was struck on three holes (BEN-SA-005, 010, 017) between 6.5 m and 8.0 m; grouting proceeded without loss.
