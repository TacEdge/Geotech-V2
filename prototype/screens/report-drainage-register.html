Two QA philosophies

As-built length and inclination are construction thresholds: outside tolerance reopens the run (DR-08). Flow at completion is a finding, zero permitted: a dry drain recorded (DR-04) is complete, never a failure. Flow is recorded once; there is no ongoing monitoring.
