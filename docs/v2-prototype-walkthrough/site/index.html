Status classifications

Each screen carries a fidelity label and an interaction label. A screen may hold both, e.g. HIGH-FIDELITY SIMULATED.

FidelityMeaning
HIGH-FIDELITYVisually mature; closely represents intended product behaviour.
MEDIUM-FIDELITYCommunicates structure and workflow; details or interactions incomplete.
CONCEPTUALIllustrates intent; not an implementation-ready specification.
InteractionMeaning
FUNCTIONALThe interaction works in the current prototype (mostly navigation).
SIMULATEDChanges local prototype state or uses mock behaviour; not production functionality.
STATICIllustrative surface with little or no working interaction (e.g. printable reports).
PARTIALSome intended behaviour exists but the flow cannot be completed.
CONTRADICTEDPrototype materially differs from a fixed Product Definition decision.
NOT REPRESENTEDThe Product Definition specifies it, but it does not appear in the prototype.

What is open to challenge

Everything the prototype illustrates about interaction and data is open to challenge; it is a clickable demonstration, not an implementation. What is not open to challenge here is the written product intent in the Product Definition — where the prototype and the Definition differ, this document records the difference rather than choosing one (see Section 11).
